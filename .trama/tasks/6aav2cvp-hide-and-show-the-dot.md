# Hide and show the dot

The dot can be moved between the screen and the menu bar in both directions. The radial menu offers Archivia, Chat, Impostazioni, Nascondi and Esci. Nascondi takes the dot from the screen into the menu bar from the resting dot, from the open menu and from the open chat; an open chat stays open under the bar. From the menu bar, Mostra brings the dot back to the screen from the menu bar icon, from the open menu under the bar and from the chat under the bar; the dot returns where it was last shown on screen.
