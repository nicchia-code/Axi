# Menu bar icon opens a menu

Clicking the menu bar icon while the dot is hidden opens a native menu with Mostra, Archivia, Chat, Impostazioni and Esci, in that order. Opening it remembers the app that was in front, so that the chosen action works in that app. Mostra brings the dot back to the screen; the other items do what they do from the radial menu.
